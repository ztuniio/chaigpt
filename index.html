Our story

Chai first. Everything else follows.

ChaiGPT started with one simple idea: chai the way it's brewed at home, poured out under strings of warm light with people who aren't in a hurry to leave.

These days it's grown into a full late-night spread: pizza, zinger and beef burgers, chicken broast, karahi, fish and loaded fries. The seating is still the same open-air setup with rattan pendant lamps overhead and bamboo screens around the edges. Come for a quick cup, stay for the whole evening.
